# employee 员工
> summary: 员工信息查询工具，支持按编号/用户ID/员工ID查询及领导链
> 引用路径: @M/v1/employee
员工查询工具

**引入**
```javascript
let employeeHelper = require("@M/v1/employee");
```
***

* [getEmployee](#getEmployee)
* [listDepartmentSuperiorNum](#listDepartmentSuperiorNum)
* [listSuperiorDepartmentSuperiorNum](#listSuperiorDepartmentSuperiorNum)

### getEmployee
查询员工信息

| **参数名**     | **必输** | **类型**  | **默认值** | **说明**   |
|-------------|--------|---------|---------|----------|
| userId      | 至少输入一种 | string  | 空       | 用户id     |
| employeeNum | 至少输入一种 | string  | 空       | 员工编号     |
| employeeId  | 至少输入一种 | Long    | 空       | 员工id     |
| ignoreStatus  | 非必输    | boolean | false   | 是否忽略员工状态 |

```javascript
employeeHelper.getEmployee({employeeNum:"CF"});
//or
employeeHelper.getEmployee({userId:"496015"});
//or
employeeHelper.getEmployee({employeeId:111111});
```
