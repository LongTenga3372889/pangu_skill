## boot-platform计算amount功能

#### 描述

*   封装了org.srm.boot.platform.amount.app.service.AmountCalculationService整个类

#### 引入

```javascript
    let amountCalculationTool = require("@M/v1/amount-calculation");
```


#### 方法列表

> * getExcludingTaxUnitPriceAndAmount
> * getExcludingTaxUnitPriceAndAmountNoCurrency
> * getTaxIncludedUnitPriceAndAmount
> * getExcludingTaxUnitPrice
> * getTaxIncludedUnitPrice
> * getExcludingAmountCalculationResult
> * getTaxIncludedAmountCalculationResult
> * getExcludingAmountCalculationResultNoQuantity
> * getTaxIncludedAmountCalculationNoQuantity
    
注意：方法入参参数参考AmountCalculationService类对应方法，且本插件所有方法参数都是对象格式传参
